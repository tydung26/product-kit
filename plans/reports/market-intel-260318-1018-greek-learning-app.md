## Strategic Insights

### What to Take

| Insight                                    | Evidence                                                                                 | Source                   |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Gamification drives daily habit formation  | Duolingo's streak system is the #1 reason users return; Drops' 5-min format also praised | Duolingo/Drops reviews   |
| Native speaker audio is non-negotiable     | Memrise and Nemo's highest-rated feature; synthetic voices are universally criticized    | Memrise/Nemo reviews     |
| Offline mode is expected for language apps | Nemo and multiple apps highlight this as key feature; travelers need it                  | App Store reviews        |
| Spaced repetition works                    | Every successful app uses some form of SRS; Pimsleur built entire method on it           | Cross-platform consensus |

### What to Avoid

| Anti-pattern                         | Why                                                                      | Evidence                                                       |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Aggressive paywall on basic features | Users uninstall; 1-star reviews specifically cite this                   | Nemo, Memrise, "Learn Greek Language" 1-star reviews           |
| Thin Greek-specific content          | Greek is underserved — courses copied from bigger languages feel generic | Duolingo reviews ("Greek course is thin")                      |
| Excessive ads covering content       | Destroys learning experience, immediate uninstalls                       | "Learn Greek Language" app: "13 ads and not one word of Greek" |
| Vocab-only without grammar           | Users plateau quickly, feel they can't actually speak                    | Drops, Nemo criticism: "no grammar, no sentences"              |

### What to Do Uniquely

| Opportunity                                     | Gap in Market                                                       | Our Angle                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Greek-first design (not multi-language adapted) | All major apps treat Greek as a side language with thin content     | Build for Greek from day 1: alphabet, pronunciation rules, grammar patterns specific to Greek       |
| Grammar integrated with context                 | No app does Greek grammar well — all skip or bolt it on             | Teach grammar through real Greek sentences, not abstract rules                                      |
| Cultural immersion + modern Greek media         | Only Memrise touches culture; no one uses Greek TV/music/news       | Integrate clips from Greek media, songs, memes, news — authentic content                            |
| Community of Greek learners                     | No app has a Greek-specific community                               | Build social features around Greek learners: challenges, voice exchange, native speaker connections |
| AI conversation practice in Greek               | Mondly's chatbot is generic; no one does Greek-specific AI dialogue | AI tutor that understands Greek grammar nuances, common mistakes by English speakers                |
